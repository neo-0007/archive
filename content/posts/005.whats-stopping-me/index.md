What is stopping me from doing something great ?
What is stopping me from building something great ?
I dont think it's the lack money or the lack of time. It's the lack of will . If you have an Idea and the will to execute it no matter what, You will eventually do something great. I recently watched Marty Supreme , A new movie and like all new movies it was a mid. But I liked how Marty Reisman never gives up , He can say he have had some struggles not us. But instead of all the problems he faced he did what he wanted to , not for the money not for the fame, even not for winning. He did cause he loved doing it and he had the will.

The key to greatness is i think "There is no key" , Just hustle , Things may not turn out the way you had planned even if you give your 100% , but should we stop trying ?
